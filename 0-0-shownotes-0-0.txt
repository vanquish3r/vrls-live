Voices In My Head

Alright for this next segment we're going to demonstrate something we all know is true…

None of us are actually in control of our lives, we're just being driven around by the dumbest thoughts imaginable.

This is called Voices In My Head, which is either an improv game or my actual daily existence, jury's still out.

Here's how this works. We have three actors whose job is just to exist physically. They cannot talk. They are basically human sims waiting for someone else to press the buttons.

Then we have their inner voices. These are their thoughts, their decision making, their confidence, their anxiety… and unfortunately also their mouth.

So whatever the voice says… that's what the character says.
Whatever the voice thinks… that's what the character thinks.
Whatever bad idea they suggest… the actor has to do it.
Which means we have essentially removed the last bit of self control these people had.

And finally we have a narrator whose job is basically to make their lives progressively more uncomfortable.  Oh - and I almost forgot to mention. The audience will help create the scenario you'll be in!

Darwin Award

March Break is supposed to be about freedom. No classes, no responsibilities, just bad sleep schedules and even worse late night food decisions. This story starts exactly where many spring break mistakes begin: 2am, a hotel hallway, and someone who is way too determined to get a snack.

So this college student walks up to a vending machine because his body has decided it urgently needs chips at an hour when nothing good ever happens. He puts in his money, presses the button, and watches his snack drop… halfway. It gets stuck. Just hanging there. Not falling. Not moving. Just existing to ruin his night.

You know the feeling. That moment where a $2 snack suddenly becomes personal. You press the button again even though you know it won't help. You tap the glass like somehow the chips respond to encouragement. You give it the little side slap like you're trying to reset its attitude. Most people stop there and accept defeat.

This man took it as a challenge.

Instead of going to the front desk or picking something else, he decides he's going to shake the machine. Which is already a bold strategy considering vending machines weigh hundreds of pounds and are basically metal rectangles full of gravity and consequences.

So he rocks it once. Nothing happens. He rocks it again. Still nothing. At this point the vending machine is basically telling him to go to bed and rethink his life. But now his pride is involved. You can almost hear his friends in the background saying, "bro just push it harder," because every terrible decision comes with unpaid motivational speakers.

So he gives it one more aggressive shove. And this is when the vending machine decides it has absolutely had enough of being disrespected.
---
The entire machine tips forward and comes down on him like it just chose violence. Suddenly this is no longer about snacks. This is about survival. This man is now pinned under what is essentially a several hundred pound metal box filled with soda and regret, all because he couldn't emotionally process losing his money to a bag of chips.

Luckily other people nearby heard the crash and rushed over to lift the machine off him. He survived with injuries instead of becoming a warning label. Which honestly might be worse, because now he has to explain this story for the rest of his life.

Imagine that conversation.

Doctor asks what happened. He has to say he lost a fight with a vending machine. Not a car accident. Not a sports injury. A vending machine. Somewhere a nurse probably had to leave the room to laugh professionally.

Chronically Online News

Welcome to Chronically Online News… the segment where we scroll through the internet so you don't have to damage your algorithm or your will to live. Some people read the news to stay informed. We read the internet to stay concerned. Let's see what humanity has been doing with unlimited access to information this week.

kitty - AI action figure trend

People are using AI to turn themselves into action figures right now, which is amazing because now you can finally see yourself as a toy nobody asked for. Everyone gives themselves impressive accessories like entrepreneur, fitness enthusiast, or CEO mindset. Meanwhile mine would just say tired, avoids phone calls, and emotionally dependent on coffee.

The accessories would just be a blanket, anxiety, a phone permanently on silent, and about 42 unfinished projects. It's basically LinkedIn but if people were honest.

Jon – The nostalgia cycle again

The internet has decided we're nostalgic for the mid-2010s now, which tells you everything about how exhausted everyone is. We've reached the point where people miss a time when the biggest online problem was which Snapchat filter to use and whether your group chat was mad at you.

We've officially reached retro internet. Give it another year and people will be talking about buffering like it was a personality trait and Vine quotes like they were Shakespeare.
---
k - AI everything

AI is absolutely everywhere right now. AI videos, AI music, AI influencers, AI assistants. At this point it feels like half the internet was written by a robot that just discovered caffeine and motivational quotes.

Honestly if AI wants to take over so badly it can start with my responsibilities. You answer my emails, you go to meetings, you deal with my stress. I'm going outside to remember what trees look like.

J - Main character energy

Another trend is people filming themselves doing completely normal things like they're in a movie montage. Walking slowly, staring out windows, holding coffee like it contains the secrets of the universe. Congratulations, you invented existing.

People say things like "I'm entering my healing era" and it's because they bought a plant and went to bed earlier once. That's not a character arc. That's just basic maintenance.

k - Rage bait still works

My favourite internet phenomenon is that obvious rage bait still works every single time. Someone can post something like "unpopular opinion, cereal with water is better" and suddenly thousands of people are ready to defend milk like it's their family legacy.

The internet has zero ability to walk away. We see nonsense and immediately decide this is now our personal battle. Someone could post "gravity is optional" and people would still write essays trying to correct them.
---
J - Oversharing culture

The internet has also decided privacy is completely optional now. People are posting their breakups, therapy progress, family drama, and medical updates like it's a Netflix documentary series.

We used to have diaries. Now we have comment sections. And strangers you met 14 seconds ago are saying "stay strong" like they're part of your support system.

k - Touch grass energy

One of my favourite trends is the internet telling people to "touch grass," which is basically modern language for please go outside and remember you're an animal with vitamin D requirements.

Someone will post a 30 tweet argument about fictional characters and the only response they'll get is "touch grass." No debate. No explanation. Just a gentle suggestion to reconnect with photosynthesis.

J - Everyone is a life coach now

The internet has also decided everyone is now a life coach. You can't scroll without someone telling you to wake up at 5am, drink water, cut toxic people, and build discipline.

Meanwhile they're 24 and filming this advice from their childhood bedroom. Your life experience is three jobs and one complicated relationship. Maybe let's slow down on the guru energy.
---
k - AI Drama Has Escalated

AI videos started innocent with cats just doing normal things like going to work or making coffee. But of course the internet escalated it. Now these AI cats are cheating on each other, getting pregnant, becoming royalty, and having full soap opera drama. I saw one where a pregnant AI cat got kicked out for farting and the comments were treating it like a real divorce.

And now it's somehow evolved into humanoid fruit doing the same thing. Bananas with abs, apples in love triangles, full Fruit Love Island drama. We really went from reality TV about messy humans to emotionally unstable produce, and honestly the chaos level hasn't changed at all.

J - Discovering obvious things again

My favourite type of content is people discovering things humans have always known and presenting it like they unlocked forbidden wisdom. Things like walking improves your mood or sleep helps your brain.

Yes. We've known this since caves. At this point someone could post that breathing helps survival and someone in the comments would say it changed their life.
