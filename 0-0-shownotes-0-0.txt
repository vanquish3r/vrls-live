Darwin Award

This happened in the US on New Year’s Eve sometime in the late 90s or early 2000s, which was a magical era where people had confidence, vibes, and absolutely no follow up thoughts.

A man was celebrating the New Year at home with friends and decided he wanted to start the year with a bang. Those were his exact words, which police later wrote down, and anytime the police quote you verbatim, it is already not going great.

Here’s the problem. He did not own fireworks.

What he did own was a handgun.

Now, at no point did his brain stop and ask why these two things were not interchangeable. It just powered forward on pure New Year’s energy. So just before midnight, he went outside, waited for the countdown, and at exactly 12:00 a.m., he pointed the gun straight up into the air and fired a celebratory shot.

Because obviously bullets know it’s a special occasion and will simply leave the planet.

Everyone cheered. The moment happened. A new year was born. He went back inside, had more drinks, celebrated, laughed about it, and fully forgot that he had just launched a piece of metal into the sky with no plan whatsoever.

---

Several minutes later, he was back outside talking with friends when he suddenly collapsed.

No argument. No warning. Just straight to the ground.

What he had forgotten, and what physics never forgets, is that bullets come back.

The round he fired went up, lost momentum, stalled, and then fell back to Earth under gravity. Not as fast as when it left the gun, but still more than fast enough to ruin someone’s entire year.

It struck him in the shoulder and lodged near his collarbone.

Doctors later confirmed that he survived because it missed his neck, spine, and major arteries by centimetres. A slightly different angle and this story ends very differently. Instead, he got emergency surgery, long term pain, and a deeply personal lesson in how time works.

Emergency responders initially assumed someone else had shot him. That made sense. Someone had clearly been shot. It took an extremely uncomfortable reconstruction of events to realise that no, this was self inflicted, just delayed.

He had been shot by himself from several minutes in the past.

---

Police reports concluded it was accidental. No charges were laid. Which feels fair, because honestly, what punishment could possibly top the knowledge that you rang in the New Year by injuring yourself with your own optimism.

Hospitals and police departments still use incidents like this every single year in New Year’s safety briefings. Falling bullets are a documented cause of injury and death worldwide during celebrations. This is not a hypothetical. This is not a myth. This is gravity doing exactly what it has always done.

So somewhere out there is a man who will forever remember the year he welcomed January first by getting shot by his own enthusiasm from the previous year.

Time travel is real. It just sucks.

🎭 Clean Slate, Dirty Secrets — Opening Setup Script

(Lights dim slightly. You step forward. Pause. Look around slowly.)

PRIEST (you):
 Ahhh… welcome, my weary sinners…
 Please—take a seat, silence your microphones, and unclench whatever guilt you’ve been carrying since January.
(Beat.)
Tonight, I stand before you not as a judge…
 but as a licensed, unqualified, absolutely unaccredited priest of poor decisions.
(Gesture dramatically.)
This is Clean Slate, Dirty Secrets—
 a sacred ceremony where you confess your pettiest, dumbest, and most harmless sins of 2025…
 so you may enter the New Year emotionally lighter and morally unchanged.
(Smile.)
Now let me be very clear about the rules—
 This is a judgment-free zone…
 except for my judgment.
 And it will be swift.
 And unnecessary.
(Beat for laugh.)

---

We are not confessing crimes.
 We are not confessing trauma.
 And we are definitely not confessing anything that would require a lawyer, a therapist, or a follow-up meeting.
Think petty.
 Think embarrassing.
 Think:
“I muted someone and never unmuted them.”
 “I said ‘brb’ and vanished for 40 minutes.”
 “I pretended my mic was broken… and it wasn’t.”
(Nod approvingly.)
Yes.
 Those are the Lord’s favorites.
(Gesture to the space in front of you.)
When you step forward, you will confess your sin aloud.

---

I will then deliver:

Dramatic judgment

Unhelpful wisdom

An absurd penance you will absolutely not complete

And finally… a blessing that cleanses your soul just enough to feel better—but not enough to grow.

(Pause. Lean in.)
You will leave forgiven…
 but not forgotten.
 Mostly by me.
 Immediately.
(Straighten up.)

So…
 Who among you is brave enough to admit they were technically the problem this year?
(Extend hand dramatically.)
Step forth, my child.
 The slate awaits.
