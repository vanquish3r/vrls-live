History of the Winter Games

Tonight, we are counting down the top 10 moments that changed these games forever. These are the big shifts that turned the games into the global show we watch today. So, grab a snack, settle into the world, and let's dive into the history books!"

1924: The Very First Games
A long time ago, there were no separate games for winter. They held a small event in France to see if people liked it. It was a massive hit! Because of that, the world decided to keep doing it every four years. It was the start of everything we love today.

1956: TV Goes Live
Before this year, you had to read about the sports in the paper. In 1956, they put the games on TV for the first time. Millions of people watched from home. It turned the athletes into big stars. It made the whole world care about winter sports at the same time.

1960: Computers Arrive
This was the first time they used computers to keep track of scores. Before this, they just used hand-held watches. They also used the very first "instant replay" on TV. It helped the judges see if a skier made a mistake. It made the games much more fair.

1980: The Miracle on Ice
This is a famous hockey story. A team of young college kids from the U.S. played against the best team in the world. Nobody thought the kids could win, but they did! It is still called the biggest upset in sports history. It showed that any team can win if they work together.

1994: The Big Split
The winter and summer games used to happen in the same year. It was way too much sports at once! In 1994, they moved the winter games to their own year. This gave winter sports more time in the spotlight. It made it much easier for fans to follow along.
---
1998: Snowboarding Joins In
For a long time, the games were very stiff and formal. Then, snowboarding was added. It brought a cool, new style to the mountain. The athletes wore baggy clothes and played loud music. It made the games feel younger and much more fun to watch.

1998: The Pros Arrive
This was the first time the best pro hockey players were allowed to play. Before this, only kids or amateurs could go. This meant the games were much faster and the hits were much harder. It turned the hockey tournament into a "best of the best" battle.

2002: New Scoring Rules
In 2002, there was a big scandal with the judges in ice skating. People were very upset because it didn't feel fair. To fix it, they made a brand new points system. Now, every jump and spin has a specific value. It made the sport much more honest for the athletes.

2024: The Backflip is Back
Backflips were banned in figure skating for over 50 years. The judges thought they were too dangerous. But in 2024, they changed the rules and made them legal. Now we see skaters doing wild flips and stunts. It has turned skating into a high-flying extreme sport.

2026: Ski Hiking (Skimo)
The games we just watched added a new sport called "Skimo." Athletes have to trek up a giant mountain on skis and then race all the way down. It is one of the hardest things a human can do. It shows that the games are still finding new ways to test people.
