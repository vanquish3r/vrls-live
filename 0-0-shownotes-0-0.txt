Darwin Award

Alright folks, gather round for our New Year’s Day Darwin Honourable Mention, brought to you by someone celebrating in a way that says “fireworks are too subtle for my vibe.” In Volusia County, Florida, right around the stroke of midnight on New Year’s Eve, someone thought it would be a fantastic idea to celebrate by firing their gun into the neighbourhood like it was the Fourth of July light show, but with more homicide risk.

Picture this. A family of four, parents, kids, the whole sleepy crew, trying to ring in 2026 by maybe dreaming of better Wi-Fi and less chaos. Instead, somewhere nearby, people decided the best way to welcome the New Year was to fire roughly thirty rounds of ammunition into the air and the ground. Not confetti, not singing, not a single balloon. Just bullets.

Now here’s the punchline. One of those bullets did not just hang out in the yard like a polite guest. No. It went right through a wall, into a bedroom, and landed under a woman’s pillow while she was sleeping. Yes, under the pillow, like someone delivered a Christmas gift that kept absolutely none of the promises on the box.

I’ll say it again. A bullet shot for celebration nestled itself under a pillow like it was offering bedtime story tips. If you are imagining a pillow fortress of fluff saving the day, that is not it. The bullet was literally just there, like a rude little souvenir of humanity’s collective judgment.

---

And here is the part that makes this a true honourable mention instead of a sad headline. No one was injured. Not the parents, not the kids, not even the houseplants. Just drywall pieces scattered on the floor like confetti gone wrong. That is not skill. That is not bravery. That is luck so ridiculous it should come with a cash prize and maybe a warning label.

The sheriff’s office reminded everyone afterwards that firing guns to celebrate is illegal and very, very bad at achieving anything other than near death experiences. Honestly, if your big New Year’s idea is letting gravity do its thing to a bullet, maybe put the gun down and pick up a sparkler instead.

So here is to the pillow bullet of 2026. The only New Year’s guest who showed up uninvited, came very close to causing absolute chaos, and still managed not to ruin the party. Consider this your yearly reminder that gravity always wins and celebratory gunfire never does.
