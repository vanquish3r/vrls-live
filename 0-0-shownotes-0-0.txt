What makes kitty growl

You know what makes Kitty growl? People who are completely unprepared for an event they have personally chosen to participate in. That is apparently my thing. And nowhere is this more obvious than the drive-thru.

You pulled into the drive-thru. Nobody kidnapped you. Nobody put a gun to your head and said, “GET IN THAT HONDA, WE’RE GOING TO BURGER KING.” You chose this. You got in line. You watched the cars ahead of you order. You slowly crept toward the giant illuminated menu displaying all of the food sold at the restaurant you deliberately drove to. And then you get to the speaker and suddenly it’s, “Uhhhhhhhhhh... can I have a minute?” A MINUTE?! What have you been doing?! You’ve been sitting in line at McDonald’s for six damn minutes! Did you think there was going to be a quiz at the end?!

And listen, it is 2026. You have a phone. I know you have a phone. It’s probably in your hand as we speak. That phone has the internet. The internet has Arby's. Arby's has a menu. Wendy’s has a menu. Popeyes has a menu. KFC has a menu. Taco Bell has a menu. Most of these places have a whole ass app where you can lovingly assemble your little meal in complete privacy without involving another human being.

You can sit in the parking lot at Sonic and debate the philosophical differences between onion rings and fries for as long as your heart desires. Take twenty minutes. Make a spreadsheet. Consult your family. Call a meeting. Compare sauces. Debate whether you really need the extra cheese. But when you pull up to the speaker at Wendy’s? ORDER THE FOOD.
---
And most of you KNOW what’s there! These menus have barely changed! Nobody pulls into McDonald's and suddenly discovers Big Macs. “Holy shit, honey! They have Big Macs here!” YES. THEY HAVE HAD THE FREAKING BIG MAC SINCE 1967. You knew that when you left your house. Nobody gets to Taco Bell and acts shocked that there are tacos. Nobody gets to Popeyes and goes, “Oh wow, chicken!” Nobody gets to Wendy’s and is stunned to discover hamburgers.

Meanwhile there are people inside standing at a POS waiting for you to complete what should have been a two-minute interaction while seventeen other things need doing because fast-food restaurants are staffed like somebody lost a bet. There are dishes. There’s food to make. There’s stuff to clean. There are delivery orders going off. There are three different timers screaming. Somebody needs something stocked. Somebody else is on break. And Brenda is at the KFC speaker going, “Do you guys have fries?” NO BRENDA, ACTUALLY WE DISCONTINUED POTATOES.

And you know what’s somehow even worse? THE PAYMENT WINDOW. Because we already told you the total. At the speaker. You heard it. You said, “Okay.” Then you drove your vehicle twenty feet. You have now arrived at the portion of the Wendy’s experience where money is traditionally exchanged for goods and services. Why, then, when the window opens, do you look SHOCKED?

“Oh! Just a second!” A SECOND FOR WHAT?! WHY IS YOUR DEBIT CARD IN THE TRUNK?! You knew this moment was coming! You had one job after the seven seconds it took you to drive from the Taco Bell speaker to the window. Locate payment. That’s it. I’m not asking you to perform surgery. Tap the card. Take the Crunchwrap. Get lost.

But no. Now we’re digging through the purse. Moving the coffee. Checking the cup holder. Opening the wallet. Going through twelve cards. “Oh, I think I have exact change.” PLEASE DON’T. PLEASE GOD. There are nine cars behind you at Popeyes and your chicken sandwich is aging in real time.
---
And honestly, at this point, why aren’t all of you ordering on the damn app? Pick the food. Customize the food. Pay for the food. Show up. Receive food. LEAVE. Technology has eliminated nearly every obstacle between you and a cheeseburger, a bucket of chicken, a Whopper, a Big Mac, a Wendy’s combo, a Crunchwrap Supreme or whatever you’re after, and somehow some of you have turned purchasing lunch into an escape room.

And while we’re talking fast food, people who order burgers during breakfast. Okay. This one might be petty. I’ll admit that. Because technically, if the restaurant says you can order a burger at breakfast, then you can order a burger at breakfast. I understand this intellectually. Emotionally? Screw your burger.

Because breakfast is set up. Eggs? Beautiful. Bacon? Ready. Hash browns? Let’s GO. And then somebody rolls in at 7:13 in the morning like, “Can I get a cheeseburger, no ketchup, no onions, extra pickles, add mayo, no mustard...” WHY ARE YOU LIKE THIS?

Now we’re dragging out stuff that wasn’t being used. Opening things. Setting things up. Using condiments that have limited hold times so we can make one lonely, weirdly customized hamburger for Steve, who woke up today and apparently decided eggs are beneath him. Again, YOU ARE ALLOWED TO DO THIS. That’s what makes it worse. I can’t even be right about being angry. I just have to stand there thinking, “I hope you enjoy your perfectly legitimate, unnecessarily complicated breakfast hamburger, you jackass.”

But it’s not even just drive-thrus. It’s people who don’t listen. You can give somebody an instruction. They will look directly at you. They will acknowledge the instruction. And then immediately do something completely different.
---
I have worked drive-thru and discovered you could probably say basically ANYTHING through that headset because people aren’t actually listening. “Welcome to Wendy's, how can we McDonald’s you today?” This one I actually did to prove the theory. They. just. start. ordering. Nothing. Not even a twitch. I could say, “Welcome to Burger King, the building is currently on fire and there’s a raccoon holding the manager hostage,” and somebody would just go, “Yeah, can I get a number three?” Nobody listens.

You know what else makes Kitty growl? People who make everything MORE CHAOTIC than it needs to be. Especially when something gets a little busy at work and somebody immediately starts yelling. WHY ARE WE YELLING?! Nothing is on fire! We got BUSY. That’s it! There are customers! Apparently customers occasionally visit businesses! Who could have predicted this?!

And while we’re on the subject of completely unnecessary chaos, can we talk about the person who waits until you post something in Discord and then immediately posts SEVENTEEN DIFFERENT THINGS underneath it? I spent time on that post. I formatted it. I made it pretty. I put the information in there. I hit send. I sit back and think, “There. People will actually see that.” And then here comes Captain Content. MESSAGE. MESSAGE. SONG. MEME. YOUTUBE VIDEO. GIF. ANOTHER SONG. RANDOM THOUGHT. PICTURE OF A CAT. SOME SHIT THAT COULD HAVE BEEN ONE MESSAGE BUT FOR SOME REASON NEEDED TO BE NINE.

And my post is now in ARCHAEOLOGY. Nobody saw it. Nobody reacted to it. It’s twelve miles up the channel being studied by historians. And THEN somebody asks a question that was ANSWERED IN THE POST. “When does it start?” IT’S IN THE POST. “Where is it?” IT’S IN THE POST. “Who’s hosting?” IT. IS. IN. THE. POST. You know what? I don’t know anymore. Maybe we’re meeting behind a Wendy’s at midnight. Figure it out.
---
And speaking of people who apparently cannot absorb information, can we talk about asking a question that has JUST BEEN ANSWERED? Not yesterday. Not last week. I mean the information is DIRECTLY ABOVE YOUR MESSAGE. You could move your eyeballs four inches north. That’s it. No research required. No Google. No app. No degree. Just SCROLL UP.

And this is really the thing, isn’t it? Apparently what makes Kitty growl is having to spend extra time on things because somebody else couldn’t be bothered to use five seconds of theirs. You didn’t look at the menu, so now everybody waits. You didn’t get your card ready, so now everybody waits. You didn’t read the post, so now somebody has to explain it again. You didn’t listen the first time, so now somebody has to repeat themselves.

And THEN there are people who don’t understand the concept of TEAMWORK. Now, I’m not even talking about Fortnite specifically because this exists EVERYWHERE. Games. Work. Communities. Projects. Whatever. There will be a plan. Everybody agrees to the plan. “Okay, this is what we’re doing.” Great. And then ONE JACKASS apparently hears, “Everybody go do whatever you want and we’ll meet back here after the consequences.”

Then something goes wrong and suddenly it’s, “GUYS?!” NO. NO “GUYS.” You LEFT THE GUYS. THE GUYS WERE OVER HERE. You cannot voluntarily piss off into the wilderness and then be surprised to discover that the team you abandoned is not somehow magically up your butt waiting to rescue you.

And while we’re talking technology for just one second, I have to say this because somebody somewhere is going to be mad about it. iPhones. I don’t get it. I had one. I did my time. I served my sentence. And yes, they’re perfectly functional phones. Congratulations. Your phone phones. But people act like owning an iPhone is a personality trait.
---
“Oh, you have an Android?” Yes. I enjoy being able to do whatever I want with the expensive computer I purchased. “But the bubbles are green.” I DON’T CARE WHAT COLOUR YOUR BUBBLES ARE. If the determining factor in our friendship is whether my text message arrives in the correct colour, we probably weren’t going to make it anyway.

And THAT is what makes Kitty growl. Actually, you know what? I’ve figured it out. It isn’t slow people. It isn’t stupid people. It isn’t confused people. Everybody gets confused. Everybody screws up. Everybody has a moment. It’s people who somehow make their complete lack of preparation everybody else’s problem.

That’s the growl. I am simply begging humanity to participate in its own damn life. Know what you want before you get to the speaker. Have your card ready when you get to the window. Read the thing before asking what the thing says. Listen when somebody is talking to you. Don’t bury the important announcement under fourteen TikToks. And if the entire team is standing over THERE, perhaps consider NOT GOING OVER HERE.

We have put computers in our pockets. We have robots in our houses. We have people hanging out together in virtual reality from opposite sides of the planet. We have billionaires launching themselves into space. We have access to basically the entire accumulated knowledge of human civilization from a rectangle we carry around in our pocket.

And Brenda is still sitting at the Taco Bell speaker going, “Uhhhhhhhhhh... do you guys have chicken?”

YES, BRENDA. BUT YOU KNOW WHO HAS MORE?

**FUCKING KFC.**
