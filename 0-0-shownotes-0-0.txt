Darwin Award

Tonight’s Darwin Award Honourable Mention goes to a South African man who went on a 10th wedding anniversary trip and decided the best way to celebrate a decade of marriage was to make sure his wife knew exactly what she’d be missing. His arm.

Now, traditionally, the 10th anniversary gift is tin or aluminium. Not trauma bonding with wildlife. But this man saw a lion enclosure and thought, “You know what would make this romantic getaway unforgettable? Turning it into a workplace safety video.”

Because apparently, he reached through a fence to pet a lion. A lion. Not a golden retriever. Not a sleepy cat in a sunbeam. A lion. The animal whose entire brand is “I kill things with my face.” And before it happened, he reportedly joked that if the lion bit him, he’d bite it back.

Sir, that is not a threat. That is meal prep talking back.

The lioness did, in fact, bite him. Because when you put your arm into the lion enclosure, that’s not affection. That’s DoorDash. He ended up needing more than 60 stitches, which is a lot, but honestly still fewer red flags than the sentence, “I’m going to pet the lion.”

And somehow he survived, so this is not a full Darwin Award. This is an Honourable Mention. A romantic little reminder for our 100th episode: after ten years of marriage, it’s important to keep the spark alive. Try flowers. Try dinner. Try a weekend away. Do not try foreplay with the food chain.
