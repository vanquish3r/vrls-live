Darwin Award

Alright everyone, settle in. Tonight’s story is a birthday themed almost-Darwin Award. Not quite a full award because somehow the universe decided to give this man one more chance. But oh boy did he try to cash that ticket.

So it’s his birthday. People are drinking. Music is loud. Everybody is having a good time. He has reached that magical level of intoxication where confidence is high and brain cells are closed for the night. And then he decides the moment needs more drama. More flair. More cinematic energy. Because nothing good has ever come from saying the words “watch this.”

He looks at his birthday cake. And instead of leaning in and blowing out the candles like a normal functioning adult, he comes up with a plan that he believes is pure genius. He is going to create a fireball to blast the candles out. Think movie trailer energy. Hero shot. Absolute legend moment.
---
So what does this man do? He grabs a lighter. Then he grabs a can of hairspray. Because when you are under the influence and in charge of fire, what better companion than a household accelerant.

He raises both tools like he is casting a spell. Everyone crowds around. Phones are out. Someone is already filming because they sense something stupid coming and they are correct. He flicks the lighter. He sprays the hairspray. And for one glorious instant he creates the most dramatic fireball of his entire life. The flames sweep across the cake, the candles go out in an instant, the crowd erupts in cheers, and he is basically the hero of his own birthday for exactly half of a second.

Then basic science taps him on the shoulder.
---
The flame races back into the can. And suddenly the hairspray transforms from beauty product to tiny rocket of destruction. The can launches itself across the living room like it has somewhere important to be. It hits the curtains. The curtains burst into flames. People are running. Drinks are spilling. Someone drops a plate of nachos. The dog is traumatized. Our birthday boy is standing there with that expression you only get when you realise you are both the cause and the solution to the emergency unfolding before you.

Thankfully nobody is hurt. The fire gets put out. The only permanent damage is the carpet, the curtains, and his reputation. Although I’m pretty sure his family gives him a very long side-eye any time he walks past a candle.

And the best part? When a reporter asked what inspired him, he said he had “watched too many action movies” and wanted an “epic birthday moment.” Sir. You delivered. It just wasn’t the flavour of epic you were aiming for.
---
So here’s to him. The birthday boy who almost rocketed himself straight into the Darwin Awards. He did not win. He lived to tell the tale. And in my opinion his continued existence is proof that dumb luck is stronger than natural selection sometimes.

Wherever he is today, I hope he is celebrating with cake, candles, and zero access to flammable products.
