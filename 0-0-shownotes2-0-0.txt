Bright Spot

Tonight’s Bright Spot is a little different, because this one isn’t about one person. It’s about the people who make this show what it is.

The VR Late Show may have a few of us up front holding mics, making jokes, and pretending we know exactly what we’re doing, but it has never been just about the people on stage. It has always been a whole weird, wonderful machine held together by people who show up, help out, build things, run things, fix things, hype things, stream things, clip things, and occasionally tolerate mildly unhinged ideas with the kind of patience usually reserved for kindergarten teachers and hostage negotiators.

So tonight, before anything else, I want to say thank you.

Static, thank you for being one of the steady forces behind this show. You’ve been part of the structure, the planning, the problem-solving, and the overall madness that helps keep this thing moving. Every show needs people who can be counted on, who understand the rhythm of what we’re building, and who help make the chaos feel like it has a purpose. You are absolutely one of those people.

Vanquish3r, thank you for being the man behind the curtain. You created and maintain the space this show runs in, and on show nights, you’re the one in the production booth making the magic happen. You hit the buttons, trigger the moments, play the videos, handle the moving parts, and somehow make everything look smooth while the rest of us are out front acting like this is all effortless. So much of what people experience during the show exists because of your work, your time, and your attention to detail.
