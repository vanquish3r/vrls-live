What Makes kitty Growl

You know what makes Kitty growl? People who are completely unprepared for an event they have personally chosen to participate in. That is apparently my thing. And nowhere is this more obvious than the drive-thru.

You pulled into the drive-thru. Nobody kidnapped you. Nobody put a gun to your head and said, “GET IN THAT HONDA, WE’RE GOING TO BURGER KING.” You chose this. You got in line. You watched the cars ahead of you order. You slowly crept toward the giant illuminated menu displaying all of the food sold at the restaurant you deliberately drove to. And then you get to the speaker and suddenly it’s, “Uhhhhhhhhhh... can I have a minute?” A MINUTE?! What have you been doing?! You’ve been sitting in line at McDonald’s for six damn minutes! Did you think there was going to be a quiz at the end?!

And listen, it is 2026. You have a phone. I know you have a phone. It’s probably in your hand as we speak. That phone has the internet. The internet has Arby's. Arby's has a menu. Wendy’s has a menu. Popeyes has a menu. KFC has a menu. Taco Bell has a menu. Most of these places have a whole ass app where you can lovingly assemble your little meal in complete privacy without involving another human being.

You can sit in the parking lot at Sonic and debate the philosophical differences between onion rings and fries for as long as your heart desires. Take twenty minutes. Make a spreadsheet. Consult your family. Call a meeting. Compare sauces. Debate whether you really need the extra cheese. But when you pull up to the speaker at Wendy’s? ORDER THE FOOD.

And you know what's even better than somebody who doesn't know what they want? Somebody who pulls up to the speaker and THEN decides it's time to consult the family group chat. “Oh, hang on, I just need to see what everybody wants.” EXCUSE ME?! You need to WHAT?! Did you drive all the way here hoping the entire family's dinner order would reveal itself to you through divine intervention?! WHY WASN'T THIS CONVERSATION HAPPENING BEFORE YOU LEFT THE HOUSE?!

And now we're waiting for little Timmy to respond, and Grandma's asking if they have fish, and Uncle Gary has seen the message but hasn't replied, and somebody's typing, and then they STOP typing, and then somebody changes their mind! WE ARE NOT HOLDING A FAMILY REFERENDUM AT THE TACO BELL SPEAKER! Pull into a parking spot! Conduct your little parliamentary session! Get everybody on the same page! THEN COME BACK AND ORDER THE FOOD!

And most of you KNOW what’s there! These menus have barely changed! Nobody pulls into McDonald's and suddenly discovers Big Macs. “Holy shit, honey! They have Big Macs here!” YES. THEY HAVE HAD THE FREAKING BIG MAC SINCE 1967. You knew that when you left your house. Nobody gets to Taco Bell and acts shocked that there are tacos. Nobody gets to Popeyes and goes, “Oh wow, chicken!”

Meanwhile there are people inside standing at a POS waiting for you to complete what should have been a two-minute interaction while seventeen other things need doing because fast-food restaurants are staffed like somebody lost a bet. There are dishes. There’s food to make. There’s stuff to clean. There are delivery orders going off. There are three different timers screaming. Somebody needs something stocked. Somebody else is on break. And Brenda is at the KFC speaker going, “Do you guys have fries?” NO BRENDA, ACTUALLY WE DISCONTINUED POTATOES.

And here's the other thing. WE ARE ON TIMERS! There are actual clocks measuring how long you're sitting there! They want those drive-thru times at SIXTY SECONDS OR LESS! And the longer you sit there contemplating the mysteries of the McNugget, the clock just keeps ticking! Seventy seconds? YELLOW. A little further into the seventies? ORANGE. Hit eighty? ANGRY RED! The screen is literally changing colours to express its disappointment in us! And somehow it's judging ME when I'm standing here ready to take your order! I'M GETTING A BAD PERFORMANCE REVIEW FROM A TRAFFIC LIGHT BECAUSE YOU CAN'T PICK A DIPPING SAUCE!

And you know what’s somehow even worse? THE PAYMENT WINDOW. Because we already told you the total. At the speaker. You heard it. You said, “Okay.” Then you drove your vehicle twenty feet. You have now arrived at the portion of the Wendy’s experience where money is traditionally exchanged for goods and services. Why, then, when the window opens, do you look SHOCKED?

“Oh! Just a second!” A SECOND FOR WHAT?! WHY IS YOUR DEBIT CARD IN THE TRUNK?! You had one job between the Taco Bell speaker and the window. Locate payment. That's it. I'm not asking you to perform surgery. Tap the card. Take the Crunchwrap. Get lost.

But no. Now we’re digging through the purse. Moving the coffee. Checking the cup holder. Opening the wallet. Going through twelve cards. “Oh, I think I have exact change.” PLEASE DON’T. PLEASE GOD. There are nine cars behind you at Popeyes and your chicken sandwich is aging in real time. And if you're the PASSENGER and you're the one paying? WHAT IS YOUR EXCUSE?! You're not even driving! You've literally had NOTHING ELSE TO DO this entire time! You could've had that card ready before we even finished taking the order! HOW ARE YOU THE LEAST PREPARED PERSON IN THE CAR?!

And THEN, when you finally pay and we hand you your food, DO YOU LEAVE? NO! Of course not! Now we're distributing six drinks to six different people! “Okay, this one's yours... no wait, that's Grandma's... who got the Sprite?” THEN we're opening the bags! Sorting out the burgers! Handing fries into the back seat! Apparently we've decided to organize the entire family dinner RIGHT HERE AT THE WINDOW! THE FOOD IS IN YOUR CAR! THE TRANSACTION IS OVER! BUT THE TIMER IS STILL TICKING! THIS IS A DRIVE-THRU, NOT A FAMILY BUFFET! TAKE YOUR FOOD AND GO!

And at this point, why aren’t all of you ordering on the damn app? Order. Pay. Pick up. LEAVE. Somehow some of you have turned purchasing lunch into an escape room.

And while we’re talking fast food, people who order burgers during breakfast. Okay. This one might be petty. I’ll admit that. Because technically, if the restaurant says you can order a burger at breakfast, then you can order a burger at breakfast. I understand this intellectually. Emotionally? Screw your burger.

Because breakfast is set up. Eggs? Beautiful. Bacon? Ready. Hash browns? Let’s GO. And then somebody rolls in at 7:13 in the morning like, “Can I get a cheeseburger, no ketchup, no onions, extra pickles, add mayo, no mustard...” WHY ARE YOU LIKE THIS?

Now we’re dragging out stuff that wasn’t being used. Opening things. Setting things up. Using condiments that have limited hold times so we can make one lonely, weirdly customized hamburger for Steve, who woke up today and apparently decided eggs are beneath him.

And I want to be VERY CLEAR about something here. This person has done ABSOLUTELY NOTHING WRONG. Nothing! They're ordering something the restaurant sells, during hours when the restaurant has agreed to sell it. They are a perfectly reasonable customer making a perfectly reasonable request. I recognize that. I respect that. I understand that there is absolutely no justification for me being irritated by this person's breakfast choices.

I STILL HATE THEIR ORDER.

That's what makes it worse! I can't even be RIGHT about being angry! I have to stand there, smiling politely, making their perfectly legitimate order, while internally thinking, “I hope you enjoy your unnecessarily complicated breakfast hamburger, you jackass.”

But it’s not even just drive-thrus. It’s people who don’t listen. You can give somebody an instruction. They will look directly at you. They will acknowledge the instruction. And then immediately do something completely different.

I have worked drive-thru and discovered you could probably say basically ANYTHING through that headset because people aren’t actually listening. “Welcome to Wendy's, how can we McDonald’s you today?” And yes, I actually said that. At a restaurant that was NEITHER Wendy's NOR McDonald's. They. just. start. ordering. Nothing. Not even a twitch. I could say, “Welcome to Burger King, the building is currently on fire and there’s a raccoon holding the manager hostage,” and somebody would just go, “Yeah, can I get a number three?” Nobody listens.

You know what else makes Kitty growl? People who make everything MORE CHAOTIC than it needs to be. Especially when something gets a little busy at work and somebody immediately starts yelling. WHY ARE WE YELLING?! Nothing is on fire! We got BUSY. That’s it! There are customers! Apparently customers occasionally visit businesses! Who could have predicted this?!

And yelling NEVER HELPS! I have never once been standing in the middle of a breakfast rush, surrounded by orders, thinking, “You know what would really improve this situation? Somebody screaming at me.” OH, THANK GOD YOU STARTED YELLING! I couldn't find the pickles until you raised your voice! Suddenly I can move twice as fast! The fryer is cooking faster! The burgers are assembling themselves! IT'S A MIRACLE!

And what exactly are we yelling about? The fries aren't ready? THEY'RE IN THE FRYER! They're cooking! There's a timer! Do you think the fries can HEAR YOU?! Do you think they're going to realize how upset you are and suddenly decide to cook faster?! “Oh, so sorry, didn't realize you were in a rush! We'll just ignore the laws of thermodynamics!”

And THAT'S what drives me absolutely INSANE! Somebody is actually trying to intimidate a basket of fries into cooking faster! WHAT IS HAPPENING?! HOW HAVE WE MADE BUYING A HAMBURGER THIS COMPLICATED?! IT'S FAST FOOD, PEOPLE! IT'S SUPPOSED TO BE FAST!

Actually, no. You know what? I've figured it out. It's not people being slow, or confused, or making mistakes. We ALL do that. It's when somebody else's complete lack of preparation, consideration, or common sense somehow becomes EVERYBODY ELSE'S PROBLEM!

That's the growl. I am simply BEGGING humanity to participate in its own damn life! THINK AHEAD! BE READY! GET YOUR FOOD AND GET OUT OF THE WAY! THAT'S IT! THAT'S ALL I'M ASKING!

I'm not asking for miracles here. I'm not asking for world peace. I'm not asking anybody to cure cancer or solve the mysteries of the universe. I'm asking you to know what you want for lunch and have twelve dollars ready when it's time to pay for it. WHY IS THAT SO DIFFICULT?!

We have put computers in our pockets. We have robots in our houses. We have people hanging out together in virtual reality from opposite sides of the planet. We have billionaires launching themselves into space. We have access to basically the entire accumulated knowledge of human civilization from a rectangle we carry around in our pocket.

And Susan is still sitting at the Taco Bell speaker going, “Uhhhhhhhhhh... do you guys have chicken?”

YES, SUSAN. BUT YOU KNOW WHO HAS MORE?

**FUCKING KFC.**
